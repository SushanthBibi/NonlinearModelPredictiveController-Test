AI for Thermal Management · Digital Twin Challenge

Predictive battery thermal management for a 40-tonne electric truck

A digital twin of a 540 kWh pack, kept in sync with its sensors, corrected by a neural network, and driven by a nonlinear model predictive controller (NMPC) that reads the route 40 minutes ahead. It is compared with a conventional rule-based BMS on the same simulated truck.

What this demo is and is not

  • All data is simulated. A "real" pack model with deliberate differences from the physics twin stands in for a truck. Parameters are engineering estimates, not measured Volvo data.
  • Where the NMPC gains: it pre-cools before the charging stop and runs the chiller gently instead of in on/off bursts, so the pack accepts more charge with less cooling energy.
  • Where the AI gains: the hybrid twin predicts 2–3× more accurately. In closed loop the gain is small here, because the Kalman filter already corrects most of the drift.
  • Trade-off: in summer the NMPC runs the pack a little warmer than the rule-based BMS, so its ageing index is slightly higher. The weights in the cost function set this balance.
